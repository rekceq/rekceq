This one was my first mature project and is supposed to be my opus magnum someday. It is a ninja game and started as a metroidvania but it drifted towards something closest to a platformer sandbox, with interactive clutter and "complex enough" physics and material properties for objects. It also have a weapon and moveset customization, with hardness and weight playing an important role in the combat feel and style. It has a good variety of traverse mechanics inspired by Mario 64 but in 2d (somersault, slide, dive, etc). The movement is fluid and smooth but the graphics are very retro inpired, using very small sprite resolution. The pallete is inspired by the SEGA Master System 3 (more precisely the pre installed Aseprite Master System Pallete) and is a nod to its relevance to the early Latino-america video game scene.
