I was born in the mountains but raised by the sea before returning to those a decade later. I played a awful lot of table top RPGs as a teenager. I still remember a campaign that lasted for about 2 years of weekly meetings and spammed to four or five generations of adventurers. I had long hair back then and was sort of a good student. I had my first PC when I was eleven, a Packard Bell Pentium 100 with 16 Mb of RAM, in 2001! I kept updated on computer tech in general since then. Specially when it comes to video games.

I started a bachelor degree in International Relations shortly after finishing (late) high school, but had to drop it two years in. Later I became a local government employee and went to university to study Anthropology. At the time I was very keen to study Biological Anthropology but the lack of support on that field made me turn my attention to Anthropology of Science and Arts. I could not come back to study after the event of the corona virus pandemic so I don't have a degree even after six years of dedication. But I got very close. When I have the chance to try again I consider doing Fine Arts or Fashion to become a stylist.

After years studying game design on my own I started learning how to code with the intent of making games. I also was a coding teacher for children on a project of my authorship when I was a public employee in my second home town. I ran for city council there too a few years ago. In the most recent relevant years I lived overseas working as a construction worker. I had to come back for reasons but I had the chance to visit several neat places and quite fewer nice folks.
